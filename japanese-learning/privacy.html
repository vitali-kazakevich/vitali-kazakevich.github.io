Japanese: learning

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

In short: the app does not collect any personal data. Your progress stays on your device, and nothing you do in the app is sent to me or to anyone else.

This policy covers the Japanese: learning app for iPhone, iPad and Mac (the “app”), developed by Vitali Kazakevich (“I”, “me”).

What stays on your device

Your learning progress — lessons, review history, review stages and study time — along with the words you add, the translations you edit and your settings, is stored only in the app’s storage on your device. It is not sent to me or to any third party. Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups, depending on your device settings.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. The app checks on your device whether your subscription is active, using Apple’s StoreKit. How Apple handles purchase data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics or advertising code, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not use the advertising identifier.

Pronunciation

Japanese pronunciation is played with the speech synthesis built into your device. The text being spoken is not sent to me.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may provide me with crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you. You can turn this off at any time in your device’s privacy settings.

Deleting your data

To erase your progress, use Settings → Reset all progress in the app, or delete the app, which removes all of its data from your device. I hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on my side.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that processes personal data — for example, syncing progress between devices — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

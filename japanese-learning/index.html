Questions, bug reports and suggestions: vitali.kazakevich.dev@gmail.com. Please mention your device and the app version, which is shown in the app under Settings → About.

Frequently asked questions

What does the free plan include?

Two vocabulary lessons and two kanji lessons a day, 10 items each. A new lesson opens once everything you have started has reached the Apprentice 2 stage. Reviews are always free and unlimited.

What does Premium add?

Unlimited lessons, your own lesson size (3 to 20 items), your own words and custom lessons, and practice of the items you keep getting wrong. Premium is an auto-renewable subscription, billed monthly or yearly.

How do I restore Premium on another device?

In the app, open Settings → Premium → Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In the app, open Settings → Premium → Manage subscription, or open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Everything you have learned stays: reviews remain free.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my progress shared between my devices?

Not in this version: each device keeps its own progress. Progress comes along when you move to a new iPhone or iPad by restoring a backup. Deleting the app deletes its progress.
